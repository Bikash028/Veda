Day 29 — Product Basket Analysis

Overview

Performed product basket analysis on the Online Retail II dataset to identify products that are frequently purchased together.

Work Completed

- Cleaned 541,910 transaction records.
- Analyzed 397,925 valid transactions.
- Identified 18,536 unique orders and 3,877 unique products.
- Created product baskets based on Invoice/Order ID.
- Generated 9,115,423 product combinations.
- Identified the Top 10 most frequently purchased product pairs.
- Created a visualization of the top product combinations.
- Identified cross-selling and product bundling opportunities.
- Developed basic product recommendation logic based on purchase patterns.
  
Key Insights
- Matching and complementary products frequently appeared together.
- Lunch bags, teacups, bags, and home products showed strong product associations.
- Frequently purchased pairs can be used for cross-selling, product bundling, and recommendations.
  
Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
  
Output
- Top product-pair analysis
- Product-pair visualization
- Business recommendations
