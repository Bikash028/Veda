# 📊 Data Visualization and Storytelling

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a data visualization story using the Sample
Superstore dataset.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into a clear and
meaningful visual story that identifies profitability patterns,
highlights potential problem areas, and provides actionable business
recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Central Business Question

**Where can Superstore improve its profitability?**

The analysis investigates profitability across regions and product
sub-categories and examines the relationship between discounting and
profit.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses the Sample Superstore dataset.

The dataset contains information including:

- Order Date
- Ship Mode
- Segment
- Country
- City
- State
- Region
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit

## 📖 Story Structure

The visual story is divided into five pages.

### Page 1 — Overall Performance

Provides an overview of business performance using key metrics:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin

**Takeaway:** Overall performance provides a baseline for identifying
areas where profitability can be improved.

---

### Page 2 — Regional Profitability

Compares profit across different geographical regions.

**Question:** Which regions generate the highest and lowest profit?

**Takeaway:** Profitability varies across regions, highlighting areas
that may require further investigation.

---

### Page 3 — Product Profitability

Analyzes profit at the sub-category level.

**Question:** Which product sub-categories contribute most to profit
and which generate low or negative returns?

**Takeaway:** Profitability differs substantially between
sub-categories, making product-level analysis important.

---

### Page 4 — Discount vs Profit

Uses a scatter plot to examine the relationship between discount and
profit.

**Question:** Is higher discount associated with weaker profitability?

**Takeaway:** Higher discount levels appear to be associated with
weaker profitability, suggesting that discounting practices should be
reviewed.

---

### Page 5 — Recommendations

The analysis leads to three main recommendations:

1. Review loss-making product sub-categories.
2. Optimize discounting practices.
3. Investigate weaker-performing regions.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis highlights the following areas:

- Profitability differs across geographical regions.
- Some product sub-categories contribute significantly more profit
  than others.
- Potential loss-making sub-categories require further investigation.
- Higher discount levels are associated with weaker profitability.
- Pricing, product mix, discounting, and regional performance should
  be investigated together when improving profitability.
