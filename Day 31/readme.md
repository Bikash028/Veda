Day 31 — Budget vs Actual Variance Analysis

Overview
Performed Budget vs Actual Variance Analysis using the sales.csv dataset to compare actual business performance against planned budget values and identify important performance gaps.

Dataset

- Rows: 4,248
- Columns: 20
- Key fields: Sales, Budget Sales, Profit, Budget Profit, COGS, Budget COGS, Date, Market, State, Product

Work Completed

- Analyzed actual and budget sales performance.
- Calculated Sales Variance and Sales Variance %.
- Calculated Profit Variance and Profit Variance %.
- Compared sales performance across different markets.
- Compared profit performance across different markets.
- Identified products with significant sales budget variances.
- Analyzed monthly actual vs budget sales trends.
- Created visualizations for budget and actual performance.
- Built a Power BI dashboard with KPIs, market comparisons, product variance, and trend analysis.
  
Key Results

Sales Performance
Market	Sales Variance	Sales Variance %
West	+32,564	+13.59%
East	+20,676	+13.09%
Central	+14,685	+5.87%
South	+5,726	+5.83%

All four markets were above their sales budgets.

Profit Performance

Market	Profit Variance	Profit Variance %
East	+2,437	+4.29%
Central	+1,272	+1.37%
West	-364	-0.49%
South	-2,562	-7.31%


Business Insights

- West recorded the largest positive sales variance.
- East performed above budget for both sales and profit.
- West exceeded its sales budget but remained slightly below its profit budget.
- South exceeded its sales budget but was significantly below its profit budget.
- Sales performance and profit performance can differ, highlighting the importance of monitoring both metrics.

Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

Conclusion

The analysis provided a clear comparison between actual and budgeted performance and highlighted markets where sales and profitability differed from expectations. The findings can support better budget monitoring and help identify areas requiring further investigation.
