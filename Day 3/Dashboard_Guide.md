# Superstore Sales Dashboard - User Guide

## Purpose

This dashboard provides a high-level view of Superstore sales
performance across products, regions, and time.

## KPI Cards

### Total Sales
Shows the total revenue generated from all orders or the currently
selected filters.

### Total Profit
Shows the total profit generated.

### Total Quantity
Shows the total number of products sold.

### Profit Margin
Shows profit as a percentage of total sales.

## Visualizations

### Monthly Sales Trend
The line chart shows how sales change over time and helps identify
increases, decreases, and seasonal patterns.

### Sales by Category
This chart compares total sales across product categories and
identifies the strongest-performing category.

### Sales by Region
This chart compares sales performance across geographical regions.

## Filters

The dashboard contains filters for:

- Region
- Category
- Segment

Selecting a filter updates the KPI cards and visualizations.

## How to Use

1. Start with the KPI cards to understand overall performance.
2. Check the monthly trend to understand sales over time.
3. Compare categories to identify strong product areas.
4. Compare regions to identify strong geographical markets.
5. Use the slicers to investigate specific segments.
