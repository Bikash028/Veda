# Day 26 — Executive KPI Dashboard

## Overview
Day 26 focused on creating a one-page Executive KPI Dashboard using Power BI.
The dashboard was designed to provide a concise management-level view of sales and profitability performance with dynamic filters.

## Objective
- Create a one-page executive dashboard.
- Define a limited set of important KPIs.
- Add useful interactive slicers.
- Visualize sales and profit trends.
- Compare regional and category performance.
- Extract key business insights.

## Dataset

Dataset: Superstore

The dashboard uses sales transaction data containing information about orders, customers, products, regions, categories, sales, and profit.

## KPIs
### Total Sales
Total revenue generated from all sales transactions.

### Total Profit
Total profit generated from all sales transactions.

### Total Orders
Distinct count of Order IDs.

### Profit Margin
Calculated using:
Profit Margin = Total Profit / Total Sales
The dashboard displays an overall profit margin of approximately 12.47% when no filters are applied.

## Dashboard Filters
The dashboard contains four dynamic slicers:
- Year
- Region
- Category
- Segment

These filters allow users to interactively analyze different portions of the dataset.

## Visualizations

### Sales & Profit Trend
Shows the change in sales and profit across years.

### Sales by Region
Compares sales performance across:
- West
- East
- Central
- South

### Sales by Category
Compares sales across:
- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

## Key Insights

1. Total sales are approximately $2.30M and total profit is approximately $286.40K.
2. Overall profit margin is approximately 12.47%.
3. West has the highest regional sales at approximately $0.73M.
4. Technology has the highest category sales at approximately $0.84M.
5. South has the lowest regional sales at approximately $0.39M.
