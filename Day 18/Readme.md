# Day 18 – Region Performance Analysis

## 📌 Overview
Day 18 focused on analyzing regional sales and profit performance using the Sample Superstore dataset in Microsoft Excel.
The objective was to compare sales and profit across different regions, calculate profit margins, rank regions based on sales, and create visualizations to understand regional business performance.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales performance by region.
- Calculate total sales for each region.
- Calculate total profit for each region.
- Rank regions based on total sales.
- Calculate profit margin for each region.
- Compare sales and profitability.
- Create regional performance charts.
- Generate business insights from the analysis.

💡 Key Business Insights
West recorded the highest total sales at ₹725,457.82.
South recorded the lowest total sales at ₹391,721.91.
West also recorded the highest total profit at ₹108,418.45.
Central generated ₹501,239.89 in sales but had a comparatively lower profit margin of 7.92%.
West had a calculated profit margin of approximately 14.95%.
Comparing sales and profit provides more information than looking at sales alone because revenue does not directly represent profitability.
