Day 30 — Regional Growth Analysis
Overview
Analyzed regional sales performance using the Superstore dataset to understand sales growth trends across different regions and identify important business patterns.

Objective
- Analyze regional sales over time.
- Calculate year-over-year sales growth.
- Compare regional performance.
- Identify high-growth and low-growth periods.
- Generate actionable business insights.
  
Dataset
Superstore Dataset
- Rows: 9,994
- Regions: Central, East, South, West
- Key columns: Order Date, Region, Sales
  
Work Completed
- Prepared the sales and order-date data.
- Created yearly sales data for each region.
- Calculated previous-year sales.
- Calculated year-over-year growth percentage.
- Compared growth performance across regions.
- Created a regional sales growth trend visualization.
- Calculated total sales by region.
- Identified business insights and recommendations.
  
Key Results
Region	Total Sales	Average Growth
West	725,457.82	20.67%
East	678,781.24	18.33%
Central	501,239.89	14.05%
South	391,721.90	10.40%


Growth Highlights
- Highest single-year growth: Central — 43.31% in 2016
- Lowest observed growth: South — -31.28% in 2015
- Highest total sales: West
- Most consistent growth: East
- Highest growth variation: South
  
Business Insights
- West showed strong overall sales performance with high average growth.
- East maintained relatively stable year-over-year growth.
- Central experienced a significant sales increase in 2016.
- South showed considerable fluctuations in regional growth.
- Regional performance should be evaluated using both total sales and growth rate.
  
Business Recommendations
- Investigate the factors contributing to West's strong performance.
- Analyze the causes of South's significant growth fluctuations.
- Study the factors behind Central's strong 2016 growth.
- Maintain and monitor East's consistent growth.
- Use both sales volume and growth rate when evaluating regional performance.
  
Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
  
Conclusion
The analysis provided a clear view of regional sales growth and highlighted differences in performance across the four regions. The findings can help businesses identify growth opportunities, investigate underperforming periods, and make better regional sales decisions.
