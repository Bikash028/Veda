# Netflix Data Analysis – Day 1

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Netflix Titles dataset to understand the content available on Netflix and identify important patterns related to movies, TV shows, genres, ratings, countries, release years, duration, and content additions over time.

Day 1 focused on data loading, cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization.

## Dataset

- Dataset: Netflix Titles Dataset
- Total Titles: 8,807
- Original Columns: 12
- Cleaned Columns: 15
- Movies: 6,131
- TV Shows: 2,676
- Unique Release Years: 74
- Unique Ratings: 15
- Unique Genres: 42
- Countries: 127

## Work Completed

- Loaded and inspected the Netflix dataset.
- Checked data types, missing values, and duplicate records.
- Cleaned missing categorical values.
- Converted `date_added` into a proper date format.
- Created `year_added` and `month_added` features.
- Extracted numerical duration values.
- Analyzed Movies vs TV Shows.
- Analyzed release-year trends.
- Performed genre analysis.
- Analyzed content ratings.
- Analyzed content by country.
- Analyzed movie duration and TV show seasons.
- Analyzed Netflix content additions over time.
- Created multiple visualizations to understand the dataset.

## Key Findings

- Movies represent approximately **69.62%** of the Netflix catalog.
- TV Shows represent approximately **30.38%**.
- **International Movies** is the most common genre with **2,752 titles**.
- **TV-MA** is the most common content rating.
- The United States has the highest number of title-country associations with **3,689**.
- The dataset contains content spanning **74 release years**.
- Netflix content additions increased significantly in recent years.
- TV Show additions reached **436 titles in 2020**.
